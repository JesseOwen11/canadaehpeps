Refund policy.

When refunds apply, how they're paid out, and the reship guarantee.

The window

Cancellation refunds have to be requested before the buy closes. At close, the count is committed to the supplier, so that's where the window ends. A request that's already open when a buy closes stays open for a few days so we can still approve or decline it — after that it closes itself and we email you.

Final-sale buys

Some buys are all sales final. That's always posted on the buy page before you pay, so there are no surprises after.

What gets refunded

A full cancellation returns the value of your order. A refund tied to an approved change returns the value of the affected items.

How refunds are paid

  • Refunds are sent as USDC on Ethereum or USDC on Solana, converted at the rate when we send.
  • You choose the coin and network, then enter the wallet address twice so typos get caught.
  • Keep the completed refund transaction ID — it's recorded with the request.
  • A submitted refund request can't be withdrawn from your account.
  • Your quantities stay reserved until an approved refund completes, then they go back into the buy.

Reship guarantee

Seized at customs, or a failed COA from a reputable lab? We reship eligible orders. Contact support with the order details and we'll sort it.

Unpaid orders

No refund needed — cancel an unpaid order from its payment page any time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Real questions, straight answers. If something's not covered here, support is a click away.

What is a group buy? +
A group buy is a limited ordering window that pools everyone's reservations to unlock batch pricing. You pay during the open window to hold your spot, and most buys ship after the window closes and the stock lands.
When does the current group buy close? +
The live buy page shows a countdown plus the fill target, and the buy closes at whichever comes first. When it hits zero, no new orders can be placed and the rules posted on that buy take effect.
How do I pay? +
Crypto only. Pick your coin and the exact network on the payment page. The full amount — including any conversion surcharge — is shown before you commit. After you send, paste the transaction ID so the payment can be matched.
Which cryptocurrencies do you accept? +
We accept Bitcoin or USDC transferred over Ethereum or Solana.Possibly more in the future.
What if I use the wrong network? +
CRITICAL: Only send tokens on the network specified at checkout. Ask for help before transfer if unsure. We cannot recover crypto sent on the wrong network.
Why do I have to paste a transaction ID? +
Everyone sends to the same published addresses, so the transaction ID is what matches one on-chain payment to one order. Paste it after sending and the order advances once the network confirms. If a valid txid won't verify, you can attach a screenshot for manual review.
Who are your vendors? +
Vendor names are available once the group buy opens.
Where do you ship? +
Orders ship from Canada. Group buys ship standard to Canada. Bulk ships Canada wide — North, South, East, West. Canada, Canada, Canada. Reach out to admins for a group buy relative to your location.
How long does shipping take? +
In-stock and bulk orders are packed once payment confirms. Group buys follow the estimate on their own page — supplier, testing, customs, and carrier timing can all move it. Tracking is emailed the moment your parcel ships.
Can I cancel or change an order? +
Unpaid orders can be cancelled from the payment page at any time. Paid orders go through the request options shown on the order page — changes and cancellation refunds must be requested before the buy closes, because at close the count is committed to the supplier. Some buys are all sales final; that's posted on the buy page before you pay.
How do refunds work? +
Approved refunds are sent as BTC, USDC on Ethereum or Solana, converted at the rate when we send. You choose the coin and network, then enter the wallet address twice so typos get caught. A submitted request can't be withdrawn, and your quantities stay reserved until the refund completes.
Are these products for human use? +
No. Everything sold here is strictly for laboratory and in-vitro research by qualified individuals — not for human or animal consumption, and no claims are made about human use. You must be 18 or older to order.
Are products tested? +
Certificates of analysis come with group buys and some bulk sales. Every published report stays in the COA library grouped by batch. Bulk orders don't include COAs by default; a custom certificate ordered as an add-on in a group buy is delivered as a private link instead of being published.

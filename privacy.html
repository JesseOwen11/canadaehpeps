Privacy policy.

The short version: we collect what an order needs, we don't sell it, and crypto-only means we never see card data.

What we collect

  • Account and contact details: email, login credentials, shipping address, phone number, and a Telegram handle — optional; enter "none" if you don't have one.
  • Order activity and on-chain payment details.
  • Refund requests include the coin, network, and wallet address you provide.

We never collect or store card data, because payment is crypto-only.

How information is used

Account and contact details are used for sign-in security, transactional email, support, fraud prevention, fulfillment, and carrier delivery. Order and payment records support inventory, accounting, payment review, changes, refunds, and customer service.

Who sees it

Only the service providers that run hosting, databases, email, security, and fulfillment receive the information needed to do those jobs. A shipping carrier or label service receives the delivery details required to move your parcel. We do not sell personal data.

Tracking privacy

We store the carrier and tracking number you see in your account and on the secure shipment page. We don't send that tracking number to any third-party tracking aggregator. If you choose "Track with carrier," your browser opens the carrier's official site, and that carrier's privacy terms apply from there.

Retention & deletion

You can request account deletion from account settings or through support. We delete or anonymize account data where possible, while retaining the minimum order, payment, security, and fulfillment records needed for accounting, dispute handling, abuse prevention, and legal compliance.
